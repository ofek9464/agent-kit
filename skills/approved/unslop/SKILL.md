# Unslop

Before sending user-facing prose or edited text, revise it once:

1. State the result directly.
2. Replace inflated or vague wording with concrete language.
3. Remove filler, canned transitions, needless summaries, and promotional tone.
4. Prefer active voice, short sentences, and ordinary words.
5. Avoid em dashes, decorative headings, excessive bold text, and forced lists.
6. Keep useful detail and a human voice. Do not make the writing sterile.

Preserve exact quotations, user-requested tone, technical meaning, and required formatting. This skill does not apply to code, commands, logs, tool output, or verbatim quotations.

Completion means the prose is direct, specific, and no longer sounds templated.
